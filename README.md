# Mantella Living World - OStim Expansion

Mantella NPCs who live through OStim scenes with you. A module of [Mantella Living World - Integration Hub](https://github.com/Abhisek571/Mantella-Living-World-Hub).

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Scene flows | Your own animation packs are built into chains of positions. A scene moves on by itself, about 25 seconds per stage, and holds on the last position until the finish or a climax. Pick a scene in OStim's menu at any time and the flow steps back for that scene. Only your two-person scenes with an opposite-sex partner. Big-guy, femdom, futa and aggressive sets, and sets such as tied, choke, sleeping, forced, vampire feeding or slave, are left out |
| Ask first | When an NPC starts a scene with you, a box asks Yes, No or Skip. Skip fades to black, half an hour passes, and it counts as done: Mantella hears it, arousal and climax counts move. Lover's Ledger and favourite positions don't count a skipped scene. Nothing starts, and no box comes, while you are fighting or a werewolf or vampire lord, while the NPC is asleep, unconscious, knocked down, paralysed, fighting or in another scene, or while a child is in the conversation; Mantella is told why. Turn the box off with "Ask before a scene" in the MCM |
| Scene talk | Your partner reacts and talks as the scene goes, and can ask for a change of position (only during sex with you). Talk chance and the gap between spoken lines are set in the MCM. The "Live scene lines" toggle turns off all live scene lines. Shy, openly excited or dirty styling needs the Arousal module. No scene lines or summary reach a conversation with a child in it. In a same-sex or group scene, positions are picked in OStim's own menu |
| Favourite positions | Each intimate partner is asked once to rate positions from her personality, then up to three favourites are kept from your scenes and told to Mantella when she joins a conversation. Favourites are picked more often |
| Bystanders | After a climax, people in the conversation who are not in the scene may comment. Nobody in any OStim scene can |
| Affection | A close NPC can kiss you (lips, neck or hand), hold hands, hug, cuddle, pat your head, dance a couple dance with you, or give or watch a dance on a chair. Either of you can ask or offer; no box asks first, clothes stay on, and it ends by itself after 20 to 60 seconds. Who does it follows the talk, so she can kiss your neck or you hers. Only hand-picked animations from OStim, Open Animations Romance and Erotica, Lovemaking Compendium, Night-blooming Violets, OBallroom Dancing, Gunslicer Dance Pack and Just Shake It are used, and only the kinds you have animations for are offered. Chair dances need a free chair close by, and the lap dance and the dance from a chair are only for NPCs intimate with you. Some kinds have animations only for some pairs: the couple dance and the lap dance only for a man and a woman, the hand kiss only for two women. When a kind works only one way round (she kisses your neck, but not you hers), the NPC is told so. Nothing starts while a child is in the conversation or while either of you can't agree (see Ask first). If it goes further and you agree to sex, the scene ends, she takes a moment, and the Ask First box comes up |
| Quiet outfits | Mantella's undress and redress events are muted during any OStim scene |
| Intimate and close NPCs only | The sex, group sex, sex between NPCs, undress, change position and rating actions are offered only to NPCs intimate with you; redress and affection only to NPCs close to you (both as the Hub decides). This module handles undress, redress and sex between NPCs itself and checks every named NPC: in the conversation, not a child, close or intimate enough, and able to agree; sex between an NPC and you goes to the Ask First box. Nothing adult is offered or done while a child is in the conversation |

Requires: [Mantella Living World - Integration Hub](https://github.com/Abhisek571/Mantella-Living-World-Hub), [Mantella - Direct AI Connect](https://github.com/Abhisek571/Mantella-Direct-AI-Connect) (stock Mantella lacks the event-only turn this module needs), [Mantella](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/98631) 0.14, Mantella's Advanced Actions turned on (Actions tab of Mantella's settings) for the position and rating actions, [OStim Standalone](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/98163) with at least one animation pack, [OSL Aroused](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/65454), [Papyrus MessageBox - SKSE NG](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/83578), [powerofthree's Papyrus Extender](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/22854), PapyrusUtil SE, SKSE. If you have [Actions For Mantella - Expanded Sex Actions](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/174770) (one file in the Files tab of Mantella - Community Resources), place this mod below it in MO2: it replaces five of that mod's action files, and placed above it the unchecked versions win. Optional: [Mantella Living World - Arousal](https://github.com/Abhisek571/Mantella-Living-World-Arousal), which gives the satisfied judgement (a new round starts after her climax if she is not satisfied), temperament styling and the "close" standing.

Build the flows: close the game and run `tools/Mantella Living World - OStim Expansion/Build Flows.cmd` once after installing and after changing animation packs. It needs Python 3 (the `py` launcher). With Mod Organizer 2 it reads the packs enabled in the current profile and creates the Flows mod; with Vortex it reads the packs deployed to the game's Data folder and makes `Documents/Mantella Living World/Mantella Living World - OStim Expansion - Flows.zip` to install with Install From File (packs are grouped by each scene's "modpack", else by folder). The game warns at launch when flows are not built. Settings: OStim's MCM, page "Living World".

Build: the Hub's `tools/build.py --only ostim`. In-game tests: `bash test/run-tests.sh` and `bash test/affection-test.sh` (DevBench).
